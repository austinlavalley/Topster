Terms & Privacy


Last updated: Sep 16, 2026


What Stays on Your Device

Your grids, your saved grids, your settings and a cache of cover art are stored on your device, in the app's own storage.
There is no account and no sign-in.
Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Search

When you search for an album, the text you type is sent to Last.fm and to Deezer, which return the matching albums. Cover images are loaded from Last.fm's servers. Those services see the request and your network address in the ordinary way any web request works, and their own privacy policies apply to what they do with it.

Usage Analytics

The app sends anonymous usage data to Amplitude, an analytics service, so we can see how the app is used and what to fix. This started with the 1.6 update in August 2026. It includes:

An identifier generated for this install. It is not your name, email, Apple ID or advertising identifier, and we cannot connect it to you.
Your device model, iOS version and language, and the network address the data was sent from, which Amplitude uses to work out an approximate location.
When the app is installed, opened and updated, and how long a session lasts.
What you do in the app, as counts and flags only: how many albums you placed, when a grid was saved or exported, which layout and export options were used, how many searches you ran and whether they found anything.

It never includes what you typed, the names of albums or artists on your grid, or the contents of a grid.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone other than Amplitude, which processes it on our behalf. Because the identifier is anonymous, there is no way for us to find and hand over one person's data on request. The way to stop it is to delete the app.

Children

Topster is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
