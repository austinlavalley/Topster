Terms & Privacy


Last updated: Sep 5, 2024


Information We Collect

Topster is designed to create and store lists of music albums locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or data from your device.

Local Storage

The app uses Apple's AppStorage to save your music album lists directly on your device.
This data is not accessible to us or any third parties.
No information is transmitted to external servers or stored in cloud services.

How We Use Information

Since we do not collect any information from you or your device, we do not use your data in any way.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share or disclose any information, as we do not collect or have access to any user data.

Data Security

All your data is stored locally on your device. The security of this data is managed by your device's built-in security features. We recommend keeping your device's operating system and our app up to date for optimal security.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
